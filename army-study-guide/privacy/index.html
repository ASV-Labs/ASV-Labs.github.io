Privacy policy / Version 1.0

Study locally.
Keep your data local.

Army Study Guide is an educational study application. Version 1.0 for Android and iOS works without an account and keeps study progress on the device.

01 Information collection

Version 1.0 does not send personal information to ASV Labs and does not include an ASV Labs account or cloud-service endpoint. The app does not request names, email addresses, precise location, financial or health information, contacts, photos, or user-generated content.

02 Data stored on your device

Study progress, favorites, theme preference, and welcome-screen preference are stored locally on the device. This information is used only to provide the app's study and progress features. Uninstalling the app may remove this local data. Device or platform backup services may separately copy app data according to the user's operating-system and backup settings; those services are controlled by their respective providers.

03 Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Version 1.0 does not use advertising networks, the Android Advertising ID, third-party analytics, cross-app tracking, or tracking SDKs.

04 Accounts and cloud sync

An account is not required. Cloud sync is not enabled in Version 1.0. If a future release introduces optional account or sync features, this policy and the applicable store disclosures will be updated before that feature is released.

05 Children's privacy

Army Study Guide is intended as a professional military education aid and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

06 Changes to this policy

Material changes will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

07 Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to contact@asaltyvet.com.
